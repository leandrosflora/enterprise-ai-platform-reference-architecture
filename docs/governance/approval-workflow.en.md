# Approval Workflow

## Objective

Ensure that corporate agents are evaluated prior to publication.

## Workflow

```text
Draft
 ↓
Technical Review
 ↓
Security Review
 ↓
LGPD Review
 ↓
Risk Assessment
 ↓
Evaluation Results
 ↓
Approval
 ↓
Published
```

## Pareceristas

- AI Architect
- Security
- Privacy
- Business Owner

## Evidence

- Assessments
- Logs
- Formal approval
