# Governance Platform

## Objective

Control the life cycle, approval, risk and compliance of AI solutions.

## Capacities

- AI Catalog
- Approval Workflow
- Risk Management
- LGPD Review
- Policy Enforcement
- Evidence Management

## Other services

- Governance Service
- Evaluation Service
- Agent Registry
- Audit Service

## Events

- governance.approved
- governance.rejected
- agent.published
- agent.retired

## KPIs

| Indicador | Other information |
|---|---|
| Approved Agents | Approved agents |
| Rejected Agents | Rejected agents |
| Review Lead Time | Average time of approval |
| High Risk Agents | Agents classified as high risk |

## Non-functional requirements

- Separation of functions
- Traceable approval
- Persistent evidence
- Integration with automatic evaluation
- Complete history of decisions
